Attendance Poll - Setup Guide

The Polls button in this app launches your pre-configured Attendance Poll in Zoom so you can count participants quickly during a meeting.

Step 1 - Create the Attendance Poll in Zoom

  1. Sign in to the Zoom web portal (zoom.us).
  2. Go to Meetings and open (or schedule) your recurring meeting.
  3. Scroll down and click "Polls/Quizzes", then "+ Create".
  4. Set the poll title to: 01 Attendance Poll
    (the "01" puts it first in the list - the app always launches the first poll in Zoom's list, so keep it at the top)
  5. Add the question: "How many in Attendance 0-6 (cohost = 0)" - Type: Single choice - Answers: 0 I am already Counted or Host / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6
  6. Click Save.

Step 2 - Add the Polls icon to the Zoom toolbar

So you can quickly access Polls during a live meeting:
  1. Start or join your Zoom meeting.
  2. In the meeting toolbar at the bottom, click the "More" button (...).
  3. Find "Polls/Quizzes" in the list.
  4. Drag it from the More menu into the main toolbar (you may need to remove icons you don't use to make room).
  5. The Polls icon will now always be visible in your toolbar.
The Polls button in this app can only click the Zoom Polls/quizzes button while it is visible in the Zoom tool bar. In a small Zoom window it can get hidden behind "More" - make the window bigger or remove icons you don't use. The same applies to Participants and Host tools (Focus mode).

Step 3 - Using the Polls button in this app

  • Click the "Polls" button in this app during a meeting.
  • It will automatically open the Polls panel in Zoom and launch your Attendance Poll.
There is a short delay (a few seconds) before the attendance count updates after participants answer - wait a moment before reading the result.
Once your poll is set up in Zoom, tick "Polls Setup" in this app's Settings so the reminder no longer appears when you press the Polls button.
