Fade To Black (FTB) - How It Works

FTB hides camera movement from viewers by covering it with a black scene. Configure the black scene name + timings here, then tick the FTB checkbox for each scene in Scene Picker.

Sequence when Cut is pressed with FTB active

  1. App switches OBS to the target scene instantly (~1 frame / 30 ms) - OBS fires the scene's PTZ action, camera starts moving immediately.
  2. App instantly cuts to the Black scene - viewers see solid black.
  3. Output holds on black for "Hold black (ms)" while the camera travels.
  4. After the hold, OBS fades from black to target using the original OBS transition. Camera has arrived - no movement visible to viewers.

Settings

Fade-to-black scene - name of the plain-black scene in OBS (e.g. "Black" or "Media"). Create it in OBS with a single black Color Source. Leave empty to disable FTB entirely.

Fade (ms) - duration of the fade-in from black to target after the hold. Longer = more time for the camera to fully settle (default 2000 ms).

Hold black (ms) - how long output stays on the black scene while the camera travels. Default 2500 ms. Increase if your PTZ camera is slow.
