Auto Join Meeting

▶ Watch: Zoom settings (video tutorial)

Let the Join Zoom button launch your recurring meeting straight in - no manual typing of the Meeting ID and no clicking through Zoom's "Join" confirmation dialog.

Where to set it up

  1. Open Settings → the Zoom tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Auto Join Meeting section.
  3. Fill out the fields below, then click Save.

Fields to fill out

  • Meeting ID - your recurring meeting's numeric ID (from your Zoom invite link, e.g. https://zoom.us/j/1234567890 → ID is 1234567890). Spaces are fine and are stripped automatically. This is required - Join Zoom shows a warning if it's left blank.
  • Display Name - the name Zoom shows for you in the meeting (optional).
  • Password - your meeting's passcode, if it has one (optional, stored masked).
  • Don't connect to audio / Turn off video - checkboxes for your preference. Zoom's join-by-URL method doesn't support passing these directly, so treat them as a reminder to click Zoom's own audio/video toggles right after joining.

How to activate it

  1. Back on the main screen, click the Join Zoom button.
  2. The app builds a zoommtg:// link from your saved Meeting ID, Display Name and Password and launches Zoom directly into that meeting - no need to open Zoom yourself first.
  3. When Zoom's own "Join" confirmation dialog appears, the app automatically finds and clicks it for you (tries for up to ~30 seconds).
  4. The button changes to In Meeting once you're connected.
If Meeting ID is left blank, clicking Join Zoom just shows a reminder to fill it out in Settings - it won't try to launch Zoom.

Want Polls to open automatically right after you join too? See Polls for the "Auto start Polls after joining Zoom" option in the same Settings tab.
