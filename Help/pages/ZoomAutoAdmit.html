Zoom Auto-Admit

Auto-Admit watches the Zoom Waiting Room during a meeting and automatically admits people you've already trusted, while anyone unrecognized still waits for you to let them in yourself. This is an experimental feature (relies on UI Automation reading Zoom's own window), so treat it as a convenience, not a security control.

How to activate it

  1. Open Settings → the Zoom tab.
  2. Check Enable Zoom Auto-Admit (experimental), then click Save.
  3. An Admit button appears on the main screen next to Focus. It stays visible only while this setting is enabled.

How it works

  • Once a Zoom meeting is detected, the app scans the Waiting Room in the background on a timer (a single scan can take anywhere from a few seconds up to a minute depending on the machine, so admits aren't always instant).
  • Anyone on your trusted list is admitted automatically the next time they're seen waiting.
  • Anyone not on the list is left alone in the Waiting Room - admit them yourself in Zoom as usual.
  • When you manually admit (or remove) someone who isn't on the list, the app notices they left the Waiting Room and asks: "'Name' just left the Waiting Room (admitted or removed in Zoom). Add to Auto-Admit list for next time?" Choosing Yes, Auto-Admit saves them so future meetings admit them without asking again.

Reading the Admit button

  • Grey - Auto-Admit is off, or no Zoom meeting is currently detected.
  • Green - armed and monitoring; the Waiting Room is currently empty.
  • Orange with a small "Waiting: N" label underneath - someone unrecognized is waiting right now and needs a manual admit.

Managing the trusted list

  1. Click the Admit button at any time to open the Zoom Auto-Admit List manager.
  2. Every known person is listed with an Auto/Manual status you can toggle - uncheck someone to stop auto-admitting them without deleting their entry.
  3. Remove entries entirely from the same dialog if you no longer want to track someone.
Because names are matched by the display name Zoom shows (normalized, e.g. ignoring "(Host)" or "- iPhone" suffixes), someone who joins under a different display name won't be recognized automatically and will need a manual admit (and a fresh "add to list?" prompt) the first time.
